# World Time
<div align="center">
  
![Screenshot of World Time](https://github.com/yeri63/World-Time/blob/master/world.png)
</div>
<p>I have friends in other states and countries, and from time to time it's nice to know what time it is there before making a call, or sending a text message, to avoid disturbing them during business hours, or late at night. That was one of the motivations for creating this application.</p>
<p>To use, just scroll down to find the city your interested in. It will display the day of the week and time, along with the difference in hours from local time. The table below shows all the cities included. Feel free to update.</p>
